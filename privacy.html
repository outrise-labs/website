Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Português

This page explains what data Outrise collects on this site, why, and what rights you have.

Who is responsible

Outrise is responsible for the data collected on outrise.technology. For any privacy question, write to ola@outrise.technology.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name (if you give it), your email, your company (optional), the optional message, the page language and the date of the request.
  • Visit statistics: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying visitors.

What we use it for

We use this data only to reply to your message and talk with you about your project. We do not sell this data or share it for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, given when you tick the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting anything done before.

Where it is stored

The form sends requests to ola@outrise.technology through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service. If we start keeping them in a database, it will be hosted in the European Union and we will update this page. The site is served by Vercel.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or at most 24 months after our last contact.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use and ask for portability by writing to ola@outrise.technology. If you believe your rights were not respected, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (cnpd.pt).
